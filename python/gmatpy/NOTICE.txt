Optional AGI/CSSI weather files, Schatten predictions and marini.dat are excluded
from the wheel and source archive. Their exclusion does not change the constants
used by the propagation convenience API. File-based weather requires user inputs.

Native platform libraries (glibc and the C++ runtime) are supplied by the
host operating system. The legacy SVN data updater and Xerces are excluded.
